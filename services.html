Serivisi Nshingiro

🛠️ Gucunga Umutungo (Inventory Management)

Dukoresha **system** zigezweho zo gucunga ibicuruzwa (Inventory) n'ibikoresho. **Intsinzi yawe:** Ibi bigabanya igihombo cy'ibicuruzwa n'amakosa mu kubara, bigatuma ubucuruzi bwawe bugira inyungu ihamye kandi yizewe.

🌐 Gukora Website, App & APK

Turakubakira website n'amapulogaramu (mobile apps/APK) ashoboye kandi akora neza bitewe n'icyo ubucuruzi bwawe bukeneye. **Turaguha uburyo bwo kuyicumbikira ku buntu (hosting free)** dukoresheje Netlify, Firebase n'ibindi, bigatuma utagira ikiguzi cy'ukwezi.

📈 Gukwamamaza ku Mbuga (Digital Marketing)

Dufasha ubucuruzi bwawe kwaguka mu gihugu no hanze yacyo. **Twemeza ko serivisi/ibicuruzwa byawe bigera ku bantu benshi bakwiye,** bikongerera igaruriro (return on investment) mu buryo bwihuse kandi bupimika.

Serivisi Zunganira

📊 Raporo Zihariye (Business Reporting)

Dukora isesengura (analysis) rihamye ry'imibare y'ubucuruzi bwawe. **Icyizere:** Dufasha gufata ibyemezo bishingiye ku makuru nyayo (data-driven decisions), bigakurinda guhomba no gukoresha ingengo y'imari ahakwiye.

🤝 IT Support Ku Buntu (Consultation)

Dutanga inama z'ubuntu (free consultation) zirebana n'ikoranabuhanga n'imbuga nkoranyambaga. **Tugushakira software cyangwa solution ikwiye ubucuruzi bwawe ku giciro cyiza cyane.**

🎓 Amasomo ya Online

Tubaka ubushobozi bw'abacuruzi n'abakozi babo mu bumenyi bwa **Digital Marketing, Financial Literacy, n'Ikoranabuhanga**. Ubumenyi bwacu bugabanya ikiguzi cy'abajyanama (consultants) kuko uba ufite ubwawe.

Tuvugishe Uyu Munsi Kuri Serivisi Zihariye

Turema ibisubizo bihagije kandi bihendutse kuri SME n'amashyirahamwe (Cooperatives). **Duhamagare utangire gukorana natwe uyu munsi.**
