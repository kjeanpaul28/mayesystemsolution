💡 Icyitegererezo: IMEI Number

IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) ni numero ihariye ya telefone yawe. Ni ngombwa kuyimenya kugirango wamenye telefone yawe irapfakirwa cyangwa niba yibwe. Code *#06# irakwereka IMEI ya telefone yawe.
