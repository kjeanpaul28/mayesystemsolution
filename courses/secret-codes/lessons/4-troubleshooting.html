Uko Gukemura Signal Mbi

  1. Koresha *#0011# kureba signal
  2. Reba RSSI (signal strength)
  3. Niba mbi, koresha *#2263#
  4. Hitamo "Automatic"
  5. Subira usuzume signal

📊 Signal Strength Guide

📶 Signal Quality

-50 to -70 dBm Nziza Cyane
-71 to -90 dBm Nziza
-91 to -110 dBm Mbi
-111+ dBm Mbi Cyane

  • • "Automatic" ni byiza
  • • Reba signal mbere na nyuma
  • • Niba signal iracya mbi, gerageza ahandi
