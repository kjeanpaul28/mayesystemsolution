Guhuza Ikoranabuhanga n'Ubucuruzi

Dutangirira He? Inkuru Yacu

MAYE SYSTEMS yavutse kubera ikibazo cyo kubura system zo gucunga Inventory zidahenze kandi zihuza n'ubumenyi bw'abacuruzi mu Rwanda. Tuzi neza ko gucunga imari n'ibikoresho mu buryo bwa kera bitera igihombo kinini cyane. Intego yacu rero ni uguhuza ubumenyi bw'imicungire y'imari na software zigezweho.

Intego Yacu (Our Mission)

Intego yacu ihamye ni ugukuraho igihombo cy'amafaranga n'igihe bitewe no kutamenya ikoranabuhanga mu bucuruzi. Tubigeraho binyuze mu gushyira mu bikorwa ibi bikurikira:

  • Gukora Systems: Gutanga Systems zo Gucunga Umutungo (Inventory) zihendutse.
  • E-Learning: Kwigisha Amasomo ya Digital Marketing na Financial Literacy binyuze muri E-Learning mu Kinyarwanda.
  • IT Support: Gutanga Inama z'Ikoranabuhanga ku buntu (Free Consultation) ku bibazo by'ibanze no gushaka Software.

Agaciro K'ibanze (Our Values)

Aka gaciro ni ko kaba intwaro yacu mu gutanga ibisubizo byizewe ku bakiriya bacu:

1. Ubworoherane (Simplicity)

Duhindura ibintu bigoye (complex systems) bikaba byoroshye gukoresha. Buri muntu wese, kabone n'iyo yaba nta bumenyi buhanitse afite, ashobora gukoresha ibisubizo byacu.

2. Kwizera N'Ubwitange (Trust & Reliability)

Dukorana n'abakiriya mu buryo bwo kwizera no gutanga ibisubizo byizewe. Tubanza kukwereka akazi kacu (Proof of Concept) mbere yo gushyira mu bikorwa umushinga wawe.

3. Ikiguzi Gito (Affordability)

Turema ibisubizo bihendutse cyane ndetse na bimwe tubitanga ku buntu (hosting free for website), kugira ngo dutange umusanzu mu iterambere rya SME mu Rwanda.
