Amategeko agenga imikoreshereze ya MAYE System

Amategeko yo gukoresha

Iyi ni amategeko agenga imikoreshereze ya MAYE System, sisitemu yo gucunga umutungo wo mu rugo. Mu kuyikoresha, wiyemeje kubahiriza ibi bikurikira:

👤

Konti yawe (Account)

Ujya wemera gutanga amakuru y'ukuri mu kwiyandikisha, kandi uzaba wenyine ushinzwe ibikorwa byose bikoreshwa kuri konti yawe.

⚖️

Ibikorwa byemewe

Ntushobora gukoresha sisitemu mu bikorwa bitari byemewe n'amategeko, cyangwa ngo ukoreshe nabi amakuru y'abandi.

🔒

Kurinda Amakuru (Privacy)

Tuzagirana ubuzima bw'amakuru yawe, kandi ntituzayohereza abandi utabifitiye uburenganzira. Wemeje Politiki y'Ubuzima Bwite iri hepfo.

🔄

Ihinduka ry'Amategeko

Amategeko aya ashobora guhinduka, kandi tuzakumenyesha niba hari ibyongereye cyangwa ibyagabanyutse mbere y'uko bitangira gukoreshwa.

Politiki y'Ubuzima Bwite

Mu gukoresha MAYE System, dukusanya amakuru yawe kugirango dukugirire neza. Aya makuru akoreshwa gusa mu gutanga serivisi yacu:

📊 Amakuru tukusanya

  • Izina ry'ukoresha/ Imeli
  • Numero ya telefone (niba uyitanze)
  • Amakuru y'ikoreshwa ry'umutungo
  • ibigizwe by'imari n'Ingano

🎯 Gukoresha amakuru

  • Gucunga no kwemeza konti yawe
  • Gutanga serivisi zose za sisitemu
  • Gutanga raporo z'imari n'Analitike
  • Gukomeza ubuzima n'umutekano bya sisitemu

🛡️ Kurinda amakuru

  • Gukoresha Encryption ku makuru y'ingenzi
  • Gukoresha serivisi za Hosting zikomeye (e.g., Firebase)
  • Kugirana amakuru mu buryo bwihariye (Strict Access Control)

🔗 Gusangira (Sharing)

  • Nta makuru tuzasangira n'abandi bantu/kampani.
  • Amakuru atangwa gusa igihe amategeko yabitegetse (byihutirwa).
  • Uburenganzira bwawe bwubahirijwe.

Inama z'Umutekano

Tugushishikariza cyane kugira umutekano w'ijambobanga ryawe n'amakuru muri rusange:

🔐

Koresha ijambobanga rikomeye

Rikomeze rikoresha inyuguti zikomeye (Upper/Lower case), imibare, n'ibimenyetso byihariye.

🚪

Siba Konti (Log Out)

Siba konti yawe igihe cyose urangije gukoresha mudasobwa y'abandi cyangwa ya rusange.

📱

Menya ibikorwa (Monitor)

Reba ibikorwa bya konti yawe buri gihe, kandi utumenyeshe vuba niba uhangayikishijwe n'umutekano.

🔄

Hindura ijambobanga kenshi

Hindura ijambobanga ryawe buri gihembwe, cyangwa niba hari igihe ushidikanyije ko ryaba ryarahishuwe.

Uburenganzira bw'Umukoresha

Ufite uburenganzira bwo gusaba ibi bikurikira ku makuru yawe:

👁️

Kureba amakuru (Access)

Urashobora gusaba kureba amakuru yawe yose twakusanyije mu gihe cy'iminsi 7 y'akazi.

✏️

Guhindura amakuru (Rectification)

Urashobora gusaba guhindura amakuru yawe yaba atari yo cyangwa adakwiriye.

🗑️

Gusiba amakuru (Erasure/Delete)

Urashobora gusaba gusiba amakuru yawe, uretse ayo dukeneye kugirana ku buryo bw'amategeko.

🚫

Guhakanira (Object)

Urashobora guhakanira ko dukoresha amakuru yawe mu buryo butandukanye n'ibivugwa muri iyi Politiki.
